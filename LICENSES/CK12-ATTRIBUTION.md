# Attribution Notice: CK-12 Foundation Source Content

## Scope

38 of the 40 documents in this corpus (all `CK12-*.json` files) reference
lesson content originally published by the **CK-12 Foundation**
(https://www.ck12.org) as part of their FlexBooks open educational
resource collection.

## What we did and did not reproduce

For each `CK12-*` document, the annotation files in `data/` contain:

- **Original to this corpus (CC BY 4.0, see LICENSE-DATA.txt):** unit
  segmentation boundaries, `relation_name` and `type` fields, nucleus/
  satellite/orbit assignments, ECU groupings, the `content_summary` field
  for each unit (an original, paraphrased description of what each unit
  contains — not a verbatim transcription), and the computed `weights`
  and `levels` fields.
- **Referenced but NOT reproduced:** the actual lesson text, image
  content, or video content from CK-12. The `source_url` field in each
  document points to the original lesson on ck12.org; users who need the
  underlying media content should retrieve it directly from CK-12 under
  the terms below.

In other words: this corpus is a **structural annotation layer** describing
the rhetorical organization of CK-12 lessons, not a redistribution of the
lessons themselves.

## Practical implications for users of this corpus

1. **Using the annotation layer alone** (relation labels, ERG structure,
   weights) for research, including commercial research tools, is governed
   by our CC BY 4.0 license on the annotations (see LICENSE-DATA.txt) —
   no NC restriction applies to the annotations themselves, since they are
   an original contribution.
2. **If you retrieve the underlying CK-12 lesson content** via the
   `source_url` fields to reconstruct full documents (e.g., for a user
   study presenting actual lesson material to participants), that content
   remains subject to CK-12's CC BY-NC 4.0 terms, and any such use must:
   - Attribute CK-12 Foundation as the source.
   - Remain non-commercial, or seek separate permission from CK-12 for
     commercial use.
